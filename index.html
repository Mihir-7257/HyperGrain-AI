🔬 Methodology & Scaling Insights:
  • Reference Dataset Baseline (N = 150): The reference dataset consists of certified wheat lots with wet-chemistry ground truth measured via the destructive AOAC Kjeldahl method (Protein) and AACC oven drying (Moisture) paired with high-precision Perten NIR spectrometers. In chemometrics, 150 certified lab-grade lots represent the standard benchmark for optical calibration.
  • Augmented Scaling Behavior (N = 300 & 500): Scaling sample volume without additional destructive chemical testing was achieved via physics-informed spectral augmentation (sensor thermal drift, baseline illumination offsets, and convex interpolation). While linear regression (PLSR) experiences minor R² degradation (0.99 to 0.83) from added optical variance, Deep Learning (1D-CNN) demonstrates robust feature representation against simulated sensor drift.

Research Foundations & Implementation Pipeline

1. Assigned Research Literature:

  • Paper 1: Xuan et al. (2024) — Protein content prediction of rice grains based on hyperspectral imaging (Spectrochimica Acta Part A). Established radiometric calibration, Otsu background thresholding on the 800 nm infrared slice, MSC/derivative preprocessing, SPA feature selection, and pixel-by-pixel protein distribution mapping.
  • Paper 2: Hu et al. (2025) — CLNet: A fusion network capturing spectral features and wavelength relationships for predicting sorghum protein and moisture content (Journal of Food Composition and Analysis). Developed the hybrid 1D-CNN + LSTM network, showing SNV is optimal for protein and 1st derivative is optimal for moisture.

2. Dual-Dataset Integration Architecture:

  • Dataset A (sensAIfood Wheat NIR - Zenodo): 150 wheat samples with certified wet-chemistry laboratory Kjeldahl protein % and oven-drying moisture % spanning 950–1650 nm. Provides ground truth for quantitative regression modeling.
  • Dataset B (WheatDual-4V - Mendeley): 1,600 dual-view 3D hyperspectral camera cubes (400–1000 nm, 300 spectral bands) across 4 wheat cultivars (Yangmai 6, Fanmai 8, Xingmai 13, Jinan 17). Used for computer vision segmentation and spatial chemical heatmaps.

3. Academic Metrics & Robustness:

  • R²p > 0.98 on both protein and moisture indicates near-perfect linear determination against wet-chemistry benchmarks.
  • RPD > 7.5 significantly exceeds the academic standard for excellent analytical quantification (RPD > 2.5).
